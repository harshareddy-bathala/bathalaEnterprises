## Design Context

### Users
- Primary users are home seekers, renters, investors, and property owners in Bengaluru (especially Electronic City).
- Secondary users are internal admins managing listings, services, and inquiries.
- Core jobs: discover trustworthy properties quickly, evaluate options confidently, and contact Bathala with minimal friction.

### Brand Personality
- Premium, trustworthy, professional.
- Emotional goals: confidence, clarity, and reassurance during high-stakes property decisions.

### Aesthetic Direction
- Light mode prioritized.
- Refined editorial look with warm neutrals and gold accent hierarchy.
- Avoid overly colorful gradients and avoid visually noisy treatments.

### Design Principles
- Use token-driven consistency for color, spacing, typography, and elevation.
- Maintain clear interaction hierarchy with predictable controls and states.
- Optimize readability and action clarity across desktop and mobile.
- Meet WCAG AA baseline for contrast, focus visibility, and control affordances.
- Prefer restrained motion that supports usability, not decoration.
